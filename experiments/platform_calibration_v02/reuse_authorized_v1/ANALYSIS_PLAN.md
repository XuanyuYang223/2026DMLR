# Calibration analysis plan

Primary endpoint: complete generated answer-plus-EOS accuracy at update 2400 on the unchanged canonical development pools. No confidence interval or population-wide family claim is planned from two shared units.

1. Retain one raw endpoint row per requested instance, unit and length (8,10,12 and pooled). Join endpoint training accuracy, development accuracy, the per-length training-majority baseline, gain over that baseline, complete-answer CE, answer CE, EOS CE, actual counts, exposure and runtime. Rejected instances receive explicit status with empty metrics, not zeros. Pooled rows weight lengths by their unchanged sample counts.
2. Publish one summary table per instance/family and length, with the mean of the two paired-unit endpoint development and training accuracies, mean training-derived majority baseline, mean within-unit gain, and the per-unit values. Also record the generation-compatible fraction and its structural exact-accuracy ceiling. A task with a ceiling below one is generation-capped; zero or below-majority scores remain visible.
3. Save all train/development curves and the selected development-best versus fixed-endpoint diagnostic. Selection never changes the primary endpoint reference. Record per-task sequence, padded, supervised and answer tokens, sample/index exposure, unique inputs, updates and elapsed time. Concurrent run elapsed sums are not wall-clock time.
4. Produce a new learnability index preserving original instance order and old statuses outside the current scope. Mark new completions as calibration on shared canonical units, preserve old six-task results as inherited, explicitly label the context-rejected instance, and retain binary/alias exclusions. No original index is overwritten. The resource table may combine inherited and new calibration rows only with their provenance stated; this is not a new set of independent repeats.
5. Source-only final audits check frozen hashes, all expected completions, initial-state identity, actual length/index schedule and exposures against the original per-seed schedule, optimizer step counts, endpoint curve/metadata consistency and checkpoint bindings. No reserved test input or model test forward is permitted. No additional model forward is needed merely to rebuild tables.
6. Reports explain that output-budget ceilings limit interpretation of structured tasks, and that all canonical lengths occur in training: per-length development scores are not unseen-length transfer. Numerical publication claims are copied from the resulting saved report/tables with exact source paths and displayed precision in the later claim ledger.

Preserve the original capacity failure and author scope decision as separate records. Completion of calibration permits the next requested stage; it does not authorize additional calibration tasks, seeds, decoding budgets or architecture tuning.
