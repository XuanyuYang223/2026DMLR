Shared CSV aggregation and selection are deferred until both workers finish. The 24-logical-CPU, 12-GB-GPU host has capacity for the two processes. Wrapper hashes and start/finish times are recorded in distinct worker summaries. Concurrent candidate runtimes overlap and include contention; their sum is accumulated run elapsed time, not wall-clock time or dedicated GPU-hours. The worker start/finish envelope supplies actual adaptation wall time.
